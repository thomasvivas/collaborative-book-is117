# Liking

## What is it?

The liking principle describes how people can be more receptive to requests
from people they like or feel connected to. Familiarity, shared interests, and
perceived similarity can contribute to that connection.

## How do you recognize or use it?

Notice whether a message builds a genuine connection through a shared purpose,
respectful conversation, or relevant common ground. Organizations can use a
warm, human voice and represent their community accurately. Avoid manufactured
friendship, pressure based on personal relationships, or implying that
similarity makes a product or claim more trustworthy by itself.

## Examples

![Illustration of a welcoming library reading-club invitation](../assets/persuasion-liking-example.svg)

- **Application example:** A neighborhood library promoting its free reading
  clubs can use a friendly invitation written in the same welcoming tone as
  its in-person staff. It can describe who the club is for and what happens at
  a meeting, so the invitation feels approachable without pretending to be a
  personal message from a friend.
