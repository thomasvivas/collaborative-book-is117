# Authority

## What is it?

Authority is the influence a person or message can have when an audience
perceives its source as knowledgeable, experienced, or legitimately responsible
for the subject. People may use expertise as a shortcut when they do not have
enough time or information to assess a decision themselves.

## How do you recognize or use it?

Look for relevant qualifications, demonstrated expertise, and clear evidence
behind a recommendation. To use authority responsibly, identify who is making
the claim, explain why their expertise fits the topic, and link to evidence.
Do not imply an endorsement or credential that does not exist, and distinguish
expert opinion from established fact.

## Examples

![Illustration of a clinician checking a cited health guide](../assets/persuasion-authority-example.svg)

- **Application example:** A public-health clinic's page about seasonal
  vaccinations can name the medical team responsible for the information, link
  to current public-health guidance, and show when the page was reviewed. The
  source's relevant expertise helps readers evaluate the advice without
  presenting authority as a substitute for evidence.
