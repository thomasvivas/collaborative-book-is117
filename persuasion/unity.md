# Unity

## What is it?

Unity is the influence that can come from a sense of shared identity: people
may be more receptive to others they see as part of the same group or
community. This is more than simply liking someone; it involves a perceived
"we" or common identity.

## How do you recognize or use it?

Look for genuine shared roles, experiences, goals, or group membership. An
organization can invite people to contribute as members of a community with a
real stake in the outcome. Be truthful about who belongs, avoid exploiting
identity or excluding people unfairly, and leave participation voluntary.

## Examples

![Illustration of neighbors working together on a shared community garden](../assets/persuasion-unity-example.svg)

- **Application example:** A neighborhood garden can invite residents to help
  plan a shared planting day by emphasizing that they are building a welcoming
  space together as neighbors. The invitation draws on a genuine shared
  community identity and explains the activity without implying that anyone
  must participate.
